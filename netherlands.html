Direct-route verification

The public results show only evidenced origin-to-final-destination services. Repeated trips are grouped into one directional route. Intermediate station combinations and transfer journeys are excluded. Review all 51 country editions and outstanding gaps in the verification report. Results show up to 50 routes per page. Use Next and Previous to browse, or export all matching rows.

Spain and France have dated Renfe and SNCF trip-level evidence. Other countries include selected official endpoint publications; a published route is not a dated departure guarantee. Country-specific verification gaps and countries without regular passenger rail are identified in the report. No claim is made to cover every operator, city or train.

Daily train averages

Where verified, the number is scheduled trains per day averaged across 24–30 October 2026. Streaming view counts the displayed direction only; the general catalogue combines both directions. Renfe and SNCF averages are calculated from their respective stored timetables, using active service dates and duplicate removal. Streaming averages count only trains that start and finish at the listed terminals; general catalogue averages also count intermediate station-pair services. Neither is a city-wide total.

Routes involving operators without complete frequency data show “Not verified.” Other countries need dated operator timetable data before an average can be supplied. Published distances, planning estimates and source-specific durations are explained in coverage notes.
